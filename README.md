# Watson's Flowers

A family co-op delivery game. The TV is Mesa and Gilbert, Arizona; every
phone is a job at Watson's Flowers on Main Street (est. 1927): drive a van
(tilt to steer, on your own phone), toss bouquets onto porches, arrange them, grow them,
dispatch, or sell roses from Irene's cart. Beat the heat, dodge the Gilbert
Goons, outrun the haboob, then ride in the Gilbert Days Parade.

Two towns, picked in the lobby: the real Mesa & Gilbert, or Rattlesnake
Ridge, a made-up town built for driving: curving roads over the hills, a
mountain pass, an airfield up on a mesa, crests to fly off, a speedway, a
container yard and a quarry pit.

Open `index.html` on the TV, scan the QR code with phones. No TV: on any
phone, "Open the shop on this phone", and the others scan its code. No phones: press
Enter and drive with the arrow keys.
